A few small controls for your checker.

How counting works

Discord’s sidebar gives unread indicators and mention counts. Newly rendered messages are counted once after a baseline scan. Counts can be lower than the true total, and old or off-screen messages cannot be counted.

Opening a channel and reading at the bottom clears its observed count. “Clear counts” only clears this extension’s view until a new unread signal or observed message arrives.

Your privacy

Selections, channel names, message IDs, and counts stay in this browser. The extension never stores message contents, your password, or a Discord account token.

Use one Discord account at a time. If an account change cannot be identified from the page, use “Forget local data” before connecting the other account.

This removes your selections and counts from this browser.
